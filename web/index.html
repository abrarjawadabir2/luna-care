Your information deserves more than a promise.

The Android project is designed for local records, but a public health app needs security verified beyond its interface.

We're making the development and release boundaries clear, instead of suggesting unfinished cloud features are ready.

  • No tracking on this pageThis static site doesn't collect cycle details, login information, or personal health records.
  • Optional services need careful reviewCloud and AI capabilities require explicit controls and a verified production backend.
  • Medical care still mattersLunaCare is educational. It cannot diagnose symptoms, prescribe treatment, or replace a clinician.

Good to know

Is LunaCare a medical device?

No. It is an educational and personal tracking project, not a diagnostic tool or a substitute for qualified medical care.

Can I track my period on this website?

Not on this page. This is an informational site. Cycle tracking lives in the Android application.

Is there a Windows or Mac edition?

Not yet. The project currently includes an Android app and this standalone informational page.
