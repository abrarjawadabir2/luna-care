LunaCare's Android project brings together private tracking and educational tools. Individual features may change as development continues.

01 / TRACK

Cycle and flow

Record period dates, flow and symptoms for a clearer picture of your personal patterns.

Keep personal notes and reflect on wellbeing in a calm, organized space.

03 / LEARN

Thoughtful education

Explore menstrual health and menstrual cup guidance, with reminders to seek clinical advice when needed.

Privacy matters

Your health notes deserve care.

The Android application is designed around on-device records and limited data sharing. Security claims and cloud integrations are being verified before wider distribution.

Important boundaries

This website does not collect cycle information, offer account registration, or send health data to an AI service. It is an informational page, not the LunaCare tracking application.
